JP Live Meeting Translator

Local PLaMo translation. No Gemini/OpenAI API key is required.
The translator tracks the interviewee's rolling caption, removes already-translated text, waits briefly for a complete utterance (about 250 ms for punctuation-ended questions, longer for unfinished captions), and sends one utterance at a time. Your own rows marked “You” are ignored even when your account name matches the interviewee's display name. In a one-to-one interview, automatic first-remote-speaker locking is convenient. In a panel interview, enter the interviewee name explicitly.
